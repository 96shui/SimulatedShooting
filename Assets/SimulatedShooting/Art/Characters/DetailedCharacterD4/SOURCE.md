# D4 teammate asset

- Source: user-supplied `Detailed Characters人物、枪械/FBX_Characters/D4_MIXAMO/D4_MIXAMO.fbx`.
- Selected character: D4, with 65 bones, helmet, vest and blue uniform texture.
- Textures: selected D4 color, normal and mask maps from the supplied `4k_textures` folder, resized to at most 1024 pixels for the VR prototype. The supplied source files remain untouched.
- Installer: `Tools > Simulated Shooting > Scene 3 > Install D4 Teammate` rebuilds the teammate visual and its animation controller while preserving `CombatActorView` bindings.
- The same `Actor_Teammate.prefab` is used for both P3 squad members. Enemy appearance remains on `Actor_Enemy.prefab`.
