# Taiwan street art

- `WeatheredFacadeTiles.png`: generated with OpenAI image generation for this project, 2026-09-14. Fictional material, not a survey of a specific building.
- `NotoSansTC.ttf`: Noto Sans Traditional Chinese, Google Fonts / Noto authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. License retained in `OFL.txt`. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanstc
- `CloudySky.hdr`: Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, Poly Haven, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky . Used only for natural sky illumination, not Taiwan location imagery.
- Shop names and street dressing are fictional environment art. They do not reproduce a specific real business or real military position.
- Ceramic facade, metal fittings, scooter shapes, water tanks, signs, drainage and combined mesh assets are generated locally by `CombatTaiwanStreetArt`.
- Concrete, mud, wood and sandbag textures reuse existing project assets with attribution in their original folders. This pass creates scene-specific materials and does not modify their source textures.

Visual references (reference only; no website photographs copied into game assets):

- Taipei arcade and pedestrian-street context: https://www.travel.taipei/file/2404/
- Taipei roadside scooter parking / arcade clearance: https://pedestriansafety.gov.taipei/Results/strategy_more?classId=270003000000000001&id=292868313686609920
- Trench materials and construction imagery: https://film.iwmcollections.org.uk/record/2713

Historical trench references inform timber, soil and drainage appearance only. The scene remains a fictional training environment, not a reconstruction of a modern real military position.
