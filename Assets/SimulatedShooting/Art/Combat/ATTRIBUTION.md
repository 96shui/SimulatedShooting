# Scene 3 resources

## Trench detail correction

- Sandbag mesh, diffuse and normal: James White (JamesWhite), https://opengameart.org/content/simple-sandbags . Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/sandbag_model.zip . Although the web page lists CC0, the included `Sandbag/License Information.txt` specifies CC BY 2.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/); retain attribution under that stricter license. Mesh axes and dimensions normalized for existing scene placements. Authored model, not a photogrammetry scan.
- Weathered wood diffuse and OpenGL normal: Amal Kumar, Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks , CC0. Downloaded 1K maps; used on trench posts, wall planks and duckboards.

- Geometry, simplified character meshes, map diagrams and Hit.wav: created procedurally within this project for this scene; no external model or audio downloads.
- Earth texture: existing Brown Mud by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven, CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud . Existing attribution: ../Textures/BrownMud/ATTRIBUTION.md.
- Concrete texture: existing Concrete Floor 01 by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven, CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_01 . Existing attribution: ../Textures/ConcreteFloor01/ATTRIBUTION.md.
- Training rifle: reuses the project's Weapon_training-rifle_Blockout.prefab; its existing assets and license records are retained. No downloaded weapon replacement.
