# Modular Factory Facade

- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/modular_factory_facade
- Author: James Ray Cock
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Downloaded variant: 4K Blend

`Source~` preserves the original downloaded package and is intentionally ignored by Unity.
Unity-ready FBX models, URP materials, and optimized textures belong in `Models`, `Materials`, and `Textures`.

The files in `Textures` are 1K runtime derivatives resized from the original 4K maps without altering their content.
