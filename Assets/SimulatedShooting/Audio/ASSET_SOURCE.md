# P1 weapon-feedback audio sources

All files below are used as spatial one-shot feedback. Audio failure never changes
weapon, ammo, hit, or session state.

## P3 enemy death cry

- File: `Voices/enemy-death-uh-ah.wav`
- Author: HaelDB
- Source: [Male Grunt/Yelling sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds)
- Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/yelling%20sounds.zip
- Original files: `yelling sounds/3grunt4.wav` and `yelling sounds/3yell7.wav`, retained under `Voices/Source~/`.
- License selected: CC0 1.0 Universal (the source offers CC0 and OGA-BY 3.0).
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Processing: same male vocalist's short grunt followed by yell; trim silence, downmix to mono, 20ms crossfade, normalize peak to 0.89, 5ms fade-in and 40ms fade-out. PCM16, 44.1kHz; no synthesized speech or gameplay damage changes.
